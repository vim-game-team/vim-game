## Problem description

When writing code, the time spent moving the cursor and navigating around a file adds up to many hours every month. Additionally, the transition from writing code, looking for a piece of code or moving your hand from the keyboard to your mouse and back just to set your cursor where it is needed, often makes a person lose their train of thought which then takes extra time to recollect.

The program known as Vim is the best solution to this problem, it has the potential to massively increase every developers efficiency when writing code via a well thought through set of key binds for lightning-fast text navigation. But due to its infamously steep and scary learning curve, most people never attempt to learn it.

---

## Solution description

We will create a web-based learning platform, that will guide the user through a fun, engaging and story-driven game, while teaching Vim at different levels. Users will be able to decide based on their preference and goals, if they want to focus on developing muscle memory, or get a general understanding of Vim. Although similar tools exist, we strongly believe that our Project will exceed alternatives, by utilizing known & effective teaching principles and focusing on the optimal balance between an exciting gaming experience, and an effective learning environment.
